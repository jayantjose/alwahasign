ABOUT

Al Waha Sign is a leading signage company based in Sharjah - United Arab Emirates, with over 18 years of experience in the industry. The company specializes in providing both artistic and functional signage solutions, helping businesses improve brand visibility, attract customers, and convey important information through various types of signs. We offer a wide range of signboard services, including customized bright 3D letter signs with ACP cladding backgrounds, LED light boxes, safety signs, graphics branding, project signs, rooftop signs, and outdoor exhibition stands.

Al Waha Sign is known for its highly skilled team, which includes experts in digital and offset printing, media rollouts, acrylic fabrication, display units, and wooden stands. The company caters to a diverse clientele, from small local businesses to large multinational corporations, and is committed to helping businesses enhance their presence through high-quality signage. Whether for interior or exterior applications, Al Waha Sign focuses on delivering innovative and effective solutions to meet the unique needs of each client.

SERVICES

Turning vision into Visibility

Sign Manufacturing

Effective signage is a powerful branding tool that leaves a lasting impression on clients. No matter your business type, well-designed signage enhances visibility, reinforces brand identity, and attracts customers. From storefront displays to indoor signs, our solutions ensure your brand stands out with professionalism and impact in any setting.

Acrylic Fabrication

Our acrylic signage offers a durable and stylish alternative to traditional glass. With exceptional depth, clarity, and strength, acrylic signs enhance lobbies, directional signage, and architectural displays. Ideal for businesses in the UAE, they create a sleek, professional look while ensuring long-lasting quality for branding and wayfinding solutions.

Digital & Offset Printing

We offer high-quality printing services for stickers, banners, and vehicle branding. Our products include vinyl, floor, outdoor, and frosted glass stickers, as well as roll-up and pop-up banners. Whether for marketing or décor, our durable and eye-catching prints ensure effective brand promotion across various surfaces and budgets.

LED Screens

Dicolor specializes in high-definition LED screen solutions for stage rentals, commercial displays, and creative installations. Our LED screens enhance events, exhibitions, and broadcasts with stunning visuals and reliability. Upholding the principle of quality, we deliver cutting-edge display technology, ensuring exceptional performance for diverse industries worldwide.

Joinery

Our joinery services specialize in crafting, fabricating, and installing high-quality wooden structures with precision and durability. With expert craftsmanship and attention to detail, we ensure strong, long-lasting products. Using top-grade materials and adhering to safety standards, we deliver functional and aesthetically pleasing solutions for residential and commercial projects.
